# Japanese Women's Leadership Initiative Website

Japanese Women's Leadership Initiative (JWLI) is a static website built using HTML and CSS for a nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering Japanese women to become leaders and create positive social change in Japan. The website highlights the organization's mission, history, leadership development programs, and partnerships. Visitors can learn about JWLI's leadership training opportunities, explore information about its founder and participating organizations, and discover ways to stay connected through newsletters and social media.

## Features

Navigation Menu: A simple navigation bar featuring links to Home, Program, JWLI, Fellows, News, Contact, and Apply.

Homepage: A landing page introducing the organization's mission of empowering Japanese women through leadership development and social innovation.

Mission Section: Highlights JWLI's goals of developing leadership skills, encouraging women's participation in Japanese society, and creating opportunities for social change.

Our History: A section providing background information about the organization's founding, development, and leadership training programs.

Who We Are: Introduces the organization's founder, Atsuko Toko Fish, and highlights partnerships with the Fish Family Foundation and Simmons College.

Newsletter Signup: Includes an email input field and submit button for visitors interested in receiving updates.

Images and Visual Content: Features photographs and organizational imagery to support the website's content and improve its visual presentation.

Footer: Includes social media links, newsletter information, and additional navigation options for exploring the organization.
